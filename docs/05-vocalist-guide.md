## Writing notes
- The leitmotif (intro strings → peak lead → over the re-drop) is the song's identity — consider echoing its rhythm in your chorus phrasing.
- Concept hook: the title suggests cosmic/void imagery (the blackened-symphonic lane rewards worldbuilding).
- The callout + final-breakdown phase 1 + false ending are engineered as the 15–20 s clip moment per 2026 single strategy.
- Blast-section phrasing tip: at 320-equivalent blast speed, vocals that subdivide SLOWER than the drums (half/quarter-time phrasing over the blast) read more powerful than trying to match the grid.
