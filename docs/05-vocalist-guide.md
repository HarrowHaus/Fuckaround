## Writing notes
- The leitmotif (heard in intro strings, the melodic peak lead, and over the final breakdown) is the song's identity — consider echoing its rhythm in your chorus phrasing.
- Concept hook: the title suggests cosmic/void imagery (the blackened-symphonic lane rewards worldbuilding — think Everblack/Helheim-tier framing).
- The callout + first 8 bars of the final breakdown are engineered as the 15–20 s clip moment per 2026 single strategy.
