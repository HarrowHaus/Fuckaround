# Archspire compositional study (song 3 input) — condensed

TEMPO CONVENTION: quoted BPM (350-440) = 2x DAW tempo; write at DAW 160-200,
16th = blast unit, 32nd = burst/gravity unit. Target: 175 core, 200 climax.

FORM (through-composed with recapitulation, 3:45-5:30): cold open at full
speed OR clean contrapuntal intro stating the THEME -> A (burst riff + blast,
vocals in <15s) -> B (string-skip/pedal lattice, different blast) -> STAB
BREAK (unison stabs, vocal gatling in gaps) -> C (harmonized twin lead) ->
GROOVE/CHUG half-time -> SOLO (sweeps) + TAP CANON (gtr1/gtr2 offset 8th,
bass 3rd voice) -> A' recap (same rhythm, ONE mutation: transpose m3/re-pedal/
8va double/half-time drums) -> interlude (THEME rubato, 2-voice harmonic
minor counterpoint) -> D climax (fastest blast) -> hard-cut outro.

RIFF GRAMMAR: 5- and 7-note 32nd bursts ending "early" (the lurch); pitch
changes BETWEEN bursts; stutter masks (XX.XX.X.) in unison with kick+bass+
vocal syllables; unison stabs at 3+3+2 / gaps of 5 or 7 16ths; string-skip
10ths; sweeps = m + dim7 shapes in sextuplets w/ tapped extension; pedal
lattice PPMPPMPM; scales: Aeolian core, harmonic minor leads/interludes,
dim7 chains, b2 stabs, chromatic enclosures inside bursts only. Recap law:
change exactly one parameter.

DRUMS (Prewett): rotate blast species every 2-4 bars (trad 16ths ->
hyper w/ constant kick -> gravity 32nds -> switch blast w/ once-per-8th
cymbal pulse); kick copies riff masks 1:1; quads (KKKK 32nds) under bursts;
china on stabs; ride bell on grooves; 1-beat 32nd tom descents; stops = zero
decay (gate cymbals). Drums mutate 2x as often as riffs.

BASS (lead): unison 8vb default; ANSWERS EVERY 2ND SILENCE with tapped
triplet dim7/chromatic fills ending a half-step from next root; one 4-8 bar
feature before recap; separation by articulation not register.

VOCALS: 16th-grid chopper bursts 1-2 bars + >=1 beat breath; accents ON
stabs, runs IN gaps; every 8-16 bars thin arrangement to stabs/chug for the
vocal gatling slot; vocals enter almost immediately.

PRODUCTION: 100% grid quantize, velocity-only humanize (+-8); true digital
silences (no tails over rests); clicky kick; mid-forward bass; at any moment
one stream fast, one sparse, one silent.
