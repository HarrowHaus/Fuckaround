# Breakdown Construction — research for the all-breakdown song (v1 of song 2)

Synthesis from Black Tongue, Bodysnatcher, Spite, Distant, Paleface Swiss, Traitors,
Bound in Fear, Falsifier, PeelingFlesh, Devourment, Lorna Shore, Whitechapel, Emmure,
Knocked Loose + producer sources (Riffhard, Nail The Mix, MetalRecording).

## Thesis
A breakdown is a grid of impacts separated by ENGINEERED EMPTINESS. The note after
silence is the loudest note in the song. Heaviness = contrast.

## Negative space taxonomy
- Micro-gap (one 16th): the default — chugs are staccato events; the dots ARE the riff.
- 8th stop: ends 2-bar sub-phrases. Quarter stop: the standard breath, every 2-4 bars.
- Half-bar stop: section seams w/ choke + feedback/callout. Full-bar silence: nuclear,
  once (must be earned by 30+s of density; >4s kills the meter unless anchored).
- Never >4 consecutive 16th chugs at <=80 BPM in the drop itself.
- Devices: full-band choke-stop (metric silence), single unaccompanied chug (section
  openers / intro convention), asymmetric dropouts (drums-only / guitar+vox callout /
  bass+drums), feedback over silence (b2 or tritone vs root), NO-CYMBAL ZONES (2-4 bars;
  when in doubt remove the cymbal).

## Grid grammar
- Anchor law: beat 1 sacred; ~70% on-grid / 30% displaced.
- Families: BOUNCE (3+3+2 cell: X..X..X.X...X...), LURCH (unequal gaps, snare on 4:
  X......X....X.O.), SLUDGE (quarter-time, <4 events/bar: O.......X.......),
  STUTTER (32nd bursts of 3-5 notes bounded by holes), SLAM (ride-bell swagger,
  swing ~55%).
- Mutation operators over 8 bars: add hit / displace hit / halve density / double into
  stutter / pitch-mutate last event / truncate (cliff). The 4th time is never the 1st.
- Pitch cells: ~90% root; b2 slide up-back; tritone slide-down origin; b9 cluster on
  final ring-outs; dead-note scrapes (#) add density without weight; pick scrape = 
  section transition marker.

## Tempo & feel
- Downtempo 60-90 half-time; quarter-time at 70-85 = snare every ~2.8-3.4s.
- Design rule: pick impact IOI first (800-1500ms heavy, 1500-3000ms CRUSHING), derive BPM.
- Tempo drops: feel-drop (free), hard step -12..18% at seams, rit -5..8% into final form.
- Late-hit feel: chugs+snare +10-25ms behind kick (kick stays grid), late hits HARDER
  (vel 120 vs 105). Swing 52-56% for bounce; sludge dead straight.

## Drums
- Kick copies guitar 1:1 sample-locked (exceptions as named events: thin under 32nd
  bursts, double under rings, one counterpoint bar per song, total dropout).
- Snare: 3 = anchor (80%); 4 = lurch; alternate-bar 3 = quarter-time; displaced once.
- China quarters = stomp clock; fewer chinas = bigger; china locked to guitar accents =
  modern downtempo; ride bell 8ths = slam; chokes ON stops; hats only in creep intros.
- Ghost notes near-zero. Fills: max 1 per 8 bars, 3-5 notes, or the REFUSED fill (rest).
- Blast-into-breakdown contrast ratio >=4:1 events/sec; import 1-2 blast bars as
  contrast before the final form.

## Guitar/bass/sub
- 85-90% single low string; dyads only on rings/final hits (5th=size, b9=rot,
  tritone=alarm). Mute depth is a 3-state parameter (choke/half/ring); rings end ON grid.
- Squeals answer on the "and" after big hits (1-3 per 8 bars). Dives on phrase-final rings.
- Bass doubles; octave pedal drone under quarter-time; carries pattern during dropouts.
- 808s: 1 per 1-2 bars (phrase 1s, THE displaced accent, rings) — NOT every hit; pitched
  sweep at seams (3-4/song); sub tail alone through stops = the 2024-26 signature.

## Movement architecture (Black Tongue model)
- Change exactly ONE dimension per movement (tempo, pitch center, density, layers,
  cymbal color, pattern family). Two+ at once only at the final form.
- Devices: tempo staircase down (2-3 steps), pitch staircase down (one-way ratchet,
  final form -2..4 st), density W-arc (valley at ~60% mark: strip everything),
  layer accumulation then strip, recurring callout as chorus substitute, FALSE ENDING
  at 75-85%, bookending (first sound = last sound).
- Final form = nastiest not busiest: lowest, slowest, sparsest grid, thickest layers,
  hardest late-feel, one disgusting signature event.
- Intro: cold full weight, or 1-4 bars feedback + lone chugs (plants the bookend motif).

## Groove psychology
- Predictable anchor + surprising gap = movement. Head-nod test: loop 2 bars, nod must
  never reset. 2-bar question/answer, one-sentence-per-section, 3-time rule (break the
  promise the 4th time), loudness last (save +1dB and deepest sub for final form).
