# THE SEVERED CROWN — Vocalist's Map

**The concept:** Archspire engine, Netherwalker soul. Rapid-fire chopper verses over
320–400-equivalent blasts, framed by tavern counterpoint, choir cinematics, and one
crown-toppling half-time breakdown. Medieval quest lyric lane: a usurped king, a
severed crown, too swift a blade.
**Key:** A minor (harmonic minor for the THEME) · **Tempo:** DAW 175 core ("350"),
87.5 breakdown, 80 rubato breath, **200 climax ("400")** · **Runtime:** ~3:05
**Track against:** `mix/the_severed_crown_vocal_ready.wav`

**The two Archspire vocal laws this song is built around:**
1. Accents land ON the band stabs; runs live IN the gaps.
2. Syllables ride the 16th grid (~11.6/sec in full bursts at 175). Write lyrics
   avoiding consonant clusters; 1–2 bar bursts, then breathe ≥1 beat.

## Writing notes
- The gatling gaps (0:42, 2:00 callout, climax stabs) are engineered as literal
  holes in the arrangement — nothing competes with you there.
- Stutter-mask bars: the kick, guitars, and bass hit the exact same mask —
  matching syllables 1:1 to that mask is THE Archspire party trick.
- Netherwalker license: theatrical variety welcomed — gutturals for the
  crownfall, shrieks for the gallop, one absurd gurgle somewhere for the culture.
