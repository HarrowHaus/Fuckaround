# Tech-death mixing (Dave Otero school) — deltas from our deathcore chain

Verified from Nail The Mix Archspire/Allegaeon sessions + DR database.

1. GUITARS: two-amp blend (6505 body + tighter/brighter layer for note
   definition); LESS gain than deathcore (~75%), quad only if sample-tight else
   double; bus: -10dB @ 200Hz (the signature — bass owns that pocket), HPF
   100, LPF ~8k, aggressive dynamic resonance suppression 1.5-8k, NO
   compression on rhythm guitars (staccato gaps ARE the articulation), mild
   downward expander to blacken gaps.
2. BASS AS LEAD: parallel layers — clean lows (LPF 250, crushed flat), grind
   HPF 250 saturated with intelligibility at 800-2k, optional 2-4k clank;
   sits 2-4dB under summed guitars, AUTOMATED +2-3dB for features; kick duck
   only <150Hz, 1-3dB, fast.
3. KICK at 300: triggered click 4-6k, low end decayed <50ms, scooped 200-500,
   CLIPPED not limited; DUCK kick 1.5-3dB during hyper 16th sections (inverse
   of deathcore), full level on impacts.
4. SNARE: sample supports real; body 150-250, keep ~350 ring, crack 4-7k;
   automate UP 1.5-3dB during blasts.
5. TRIPLE PARALLEL DRUM COMP (the signature): three buses, all slow attack /
   fast release — Compex-style (snare over unity, toms, OH), Decomp-style
   (kick+kit, sidechain HPF), Devil-Loc squash (snare) — blended -10/-12/-15
   under dry. Gate AFTER the crush. NO ROOM MICS; tiny 0.3-0.6s dense room IR
   at whisper level instead.
6. DENSITY: one lead voice at a time enforced by automation; arrangement-
   dependent dynamic EQ (guitars dip 900-1.5k keyed by bass grind when bass
   features); mono <80Hz, width added only >600Hz; sacrifice low-mid warmth,
   rooms, tails — articulation over weight.
7. MASTER: Archspire ships DR3 (~-5 LUFS!); sane target -7 LUFS-I, TP -1;
   distributed clipping (kick clip -> drum bus clip -> tape sat -> limiter
   doing only 3-5dB); brightness tilt +1-2dB shelf above 2.5k vs deathcore;
   verbs <=1.0s everywhere, sends automated per section.
