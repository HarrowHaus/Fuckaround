# MySpace deathcore revival brief (condensed)
Scene 2024-26: PSYCHO-FRAME (manifesto: NO SYMPHONIES / NO PUSH PITS / NO
WHISPER VOCALS / NO GIMMICKS), Tracheotomy, Thus Spoke Zarathustra, Girl of
Glass, Peacemaker FL, katywentmissing, VICTIMBLAMED, CRUCIFICTION + OG bands
re-brutalizing (Whitechapel Hymns in Dissonance 2025). DIY hardcore energy,
raw-on-purpose 2007 production, EPs, songs 2:00-3:30.

Era guitar law (2006-2010): tunings Drop C..Drop A on 6/7 (NO 8-string);
chromatic 0-1-0/0-1-2 low-string riffs; AtG-derived tremolo minor runs (the
melody lives here); pinch squeal punctuation (not whammy dives); breakdowns on
quarter/8th grids, variation by REMOVING notes, 2-4 bar phrases; slam scoop
max one section; two-step/mosh part (the hardcore inheritance); vocal-driven
simplicity (gang shout, a-cappella scream pickup, bree call-and-answer);
sample-clip intro; structure: blast-in -> chromatic verse -> two-step ->
tremolo verse -> mid breakdown -> tremolo chorus/bridge -> a-cappella pickup
-> slower final breakdown w/ squeals -> dead stop. Solos rare/chaotic.

Banned: 8-strings/drop E-F#, symphonic stems, sub-drop impacts, whisper vox,
polymetric/triplet-stutter breakdowns, 5+ min arcs, pristine modern mixes.

Hybrid pitch: a 2007 Carnifex/early-SS song that happens to be about knights —
Netherwalker's orchestra demoted to tremolo guitar melody in Drop A#; every
cinematic urge answered with a breakdown. Fantasy survives as WRITING, not
production. Lore intro as a mono lo-fi movie-sample clip; optional <=20s
tavern-lute cold open cut off by the blast-in.
