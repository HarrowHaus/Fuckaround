# The State of Deathcore Songwriting, 2024 – mid-2026

Research agent report (compiled 2026-08-01). Sources cited inline.

## 1. Major releases and their deltas

- **Lorna Shore — I Feel the Everblack Festering Within Me** (Sept 2025): 10 tracks / 66:27, no guests, #1 rock/metal in UK/DE/SE. Delta vs Pain Remains: denser symphonics BUT vocals pushed forward, shorter minimum song length ("Unbreakable" 4:49), overt melodic/anthemic writing — "arena extreme metal." Dissenting takes matter: "checklist construction," each track trying to "outdo each prior cut in breakdown weight" — refinement not innovation.
- **Whitechapel — Hymns in Dissonance** (Mar 2025): concept album (cult leader, seven-deadly-sins hymns), first self-produced (Householder). Sharpest reversal of the window: **zero clean vocals** after two clean-heavy albums; **drop G, dropping to drop F for breakdowns** (lowest ever). THE flagship data point for old-school reversion.
- **Shadow of Intent — Imperium Delirium** (June 2025): self-released, Wiseman-produced, Corpsegrinder feature. Re-deathcore-ification after melodeath drift; songwriting compression (most tracks 3:51–4:30); "tried, true, predictable" critique.
- **Slaughter to Prevail — Grizzly** (July 2025): genre line literally "deathcore, nu metal"; Radke + Babymetal features; tracks 2:22–4:53 (most radio-shaped major release); Slipknot-groove structural. Biggest band in genre (~1.2M monthly). Reception split hard.
- **Fit for an Autopsy — The Nothing That Is** (Oct 2024): increased clean singing incl. full clean chorus + piano outro; Gojira-inflected "thinking man's deathcore."
- **Signs of the Swarm — To Rid Myself of Truth** (Aug 2025): Meshuggah/Gojira/Fear Factory absorbed; 4 guests on 11 tracks; streamlining panned by AMG, praised by Blabbermouth.
- **Enterprise Earth — Death: An Anthology** (2024): tech-leaning, melodic-solo-heavy; separate instrumental edition (growing product format).
- **Ov Sulfur — Endless** (Jan 2026): doubled-down cleans, "most unexpectedly melodic brutality in recent memory."
- **Worm Shepherd — Hunger** (2024) + **Dawn of the Iconoclast EP** (2026): textbook symphonic/blackened.
- **Mental Cruelty** 2025-26 singles: Norse cycle, folk metal + melodeath + talharpa.
- **Brand of Sacrifice**: LP3 sessions wrapped July 2025; double single June 2026 (Nuclear Blast).
- **Distant — Into Despair** (announced Nov 2026): downtempo/dissonant identity, "most personal work."
- **Breakout newcomers**: PSYCHO-FRAME (2007-08 callback, "hottest new band"); Left to Suffer (nu-deathcore archetype, rap cadences, ~412K); Disembodied Tyrant × Synestia *The Poetic Edda* (baroque × techdeath, Vivaldi's Winter reimagined; two-man intercontinental studio project — the producer-duo model displacing the five-piece); PeelingFlesh (rap-slam); Darko US (synth/djent duo); Bodysnatcher (beatdown fusion, "breakdowns just don't stop").

## 2. Scene-wide trends

**The Great Divide** (most-cited 2025-26 framing): symphonic/arena wing (Lorna Shore, SOI) vs an underground counter-reaction of "raw, ignorant, groovy heaviness" (Left to Suffer, PSYCHO-FRAME, PeelingFlesh, Bodysnatcher). Heavy Blog 2025 retrospective: fan reception "tipped back in favour of the more old-school sound... or the beatdown side"; symphonic persists "with less dominance." Whitechapel going clean-free = establishment ratifying the swing.

**Nu-deathcore**: Alpha Wolf *Half Living Things* (2024) mainstreamed it (turntablism, Slipknot/Limp Bizkit signifiers) framed as escape from deathcore formula. Ten56. *IO* (2025) doubles down (industrial/downtempo). Paleface Swiss now 3rd-biggest adjacent act (~891K) — *Cursed* (2025) split critics, keeps growing.

**Rap-slam / G-code**: PeelingFlesh *The G Code* (2024) = movement. "Slam is gangsta rap": streetwear, grills, news/viral samples, rap cadences, 808s, DJ drops, skits. Part of broader slam+beatdown resurgence among young audiences.

**Knocked Loose's gravitational pull**: #23 Billboard 200, Grammy nom ("Suffocate" ft. Poppy). Panic chords (dissonant two-string skronk stabs), abrupt tempo drops, reggaeton-inspired breakdown. Mid-2020s deathcore breakdowns increasingly use hardcore-derived dissonant accents and rhythmic misdirection over pure chugs.

**Downtempo/beatdown bleed**: Black Tongue's half-time doom template everywhere (Distant, Ten56., Bodysnatcher, Paleface) — breakdown-as-song-body, not breakdown-as-event.

**Cleans: adopted then contested**. Normal in melodic/symphonic wing (FFAA, Ov Sulfur, Lorna's melodic turn); essentially BANNED in nu-deathcore/beatdown/slam wing (Whitechapel removed them on purpose).

**Symphonic post-Lorna: saturated, not collapsed**. Persisting "with less dominance"; leaders reviewed with "formulaic/predictable/checklist" language — critical fatigue, not commercial decline.

**Electronic/industrial**: BoS = template (choirs + world instruments + synth drops); Darko US synth/djent; Ten56. industrial; PeelingFlesh wholesale hip-hop production. Glitch/trap-tail production on breakdowns = standard flourish in the younger wing.

## 3. Structure and parameters

**Runtimes bifurcate**: symphonic wing averages 6:39/track (Lorna); mainstream-brutal wing 3:51–4:30 (SOI), 2:22–4:53 (Grizzly), 3–5 min (Whitechapel); slam/beatdown 2–3 min. The 7–10 min multi-movement epic is now almost exclusively an album-closer convention ("Forevermore" 9:47; SOI title track 7:34).

**Tunings**: center of gravity sunk from 2007's drop C/B to **drop A–G# baseline**, drop G/F#/F common in the brutal wing, drop E on 8-strings as effective floor; 9-string niche. Whitechapel: drop G, **drop F specifically for breakdowns** (a tuning drop as a section event — press-worthy in 2025).

**Tempo behavior**: oscillation between blasts/tremolo (symphonic), 120–160 double-kick grooves, ~60–80 half-time breakdowns. Breakdown escalation = organizing principle in the symphonic wing; beatdown wing inverts the ratio (slow riff IS the song).

**Breakdown placement**: (a) classic final-third payoff with vocal callout + tuning drop; (b) nu/slam wing FRONT-LOADS breakdowns in the first 60 seconds (playlist skip-rates punish slow builds).

**Intros/outros**: symphonic = cinematic orchestral intros/interludes standard; slam = sampled intros (news clips, viral videos); closers = long-form epic or piano/clean comedown.

**Features near-obligatory**: SOTS 4-on-11; STP Radke+Babymetal; SOI Corpsegrinder; cross-genre features (Babymetal, Radke, Poppy) mark this era. Lorna Shore = the feature-free holdout.

**Singles-first economy**: Grizzly = 7 pre-release singles over 3 years; SOI 5-of-12 pre-released; BoS returned via double single; Mental Cruelty year-plus singles campaign with no LP. Counterweight: concept album persists at the top (album-as-statement for legacy acts, single-as-unit for everyone else).

## 4. Riff language: 2026 vs 2020 vs 2007

- **2007**: drop C/B, tremolo + breakdown, 0-0-0 chugs + pinch harmonics, breakdown as discrete song-ending event.
- **2020**: symphonic-blackened — blast-backed tremolo melody, orchestral counterlines, polyrhythmic chugs, ride-the-riff breakdowns with cymbal-choke stutters.
- **2026 — three dialects**:
  1. **Symphonic-maximal**: polyrhythmic low-string patterns under orchestral top-lines, polymeter serving song, sweep/neoclassical inserts (literal Vivaldi), tremolo as melody-carrier.
  2. **Groove-ignorant (nu/slam)**: 2–4-note riffs in drop G–E built on RHYTHMIC DISPLACEMENT rather than pitch content; Slipknot bounce; rap-cadence vocal rhythms driving the accent grid; 808-doubled hits.
  3. **Dissonant-hardcore hybrid**: panic-chord stabs and skronk voicings interrupting chugs; Meshuggah/Fear Factory machine syncopation; "dissonance" as title-level brand.
- **Stutter/glitch editing = the production-as-composition shift**: a 2007 riff was a guitar part; a 2020 riff was a guitar part with orchestration; **a 2026 riff is an arrangement object** — guitar + sub-drop + vocal chop + edit — written for both the pit and the phone speaker.

**Net assessment**: genre is commercially the biggest ever (three acts near/above 900K monthlies), compositionally split: symphonic wing consolidating under critical fatigue; growth energy and new riff language coming from nu-deathcore/beatdown/rap-slam underground + Knocked Loose's dissonance vocabulary crossing over.
