# Extreme Deathcore Drum Programming — 2025/26 "Olympics" Tier (v3 research)

Grids verified against Songsterr community/artist transcriptions (Lorna Shore, STP,
Signs of the Swarm, Brand of Sacrifice, Shadow of Intent, Mental Cruelty),
Wikipedia blast taxonomy, DRUM! Magazine, Nail The Mix / URM production sources.

## The core finding
Elite deathcore is written at its HALF-TIME breakdown tempo (120-165 BPM) with
blasts as 32nds and hyper-kicks as 32nd triplets. "280 BPM blast" = combined
kick+snare roll = 32nds in a 140 session. Convert: quoted blast BPM = 2 x session BPM.

## Blast taxonomy (per-limb, 32nd-slot grids)
- TRADITIONAL (verse blast): kick every 16th (KICK-LED), snare every odd 32nd
  (+1/32 offset), ride 8ths in unison with kick. 240-290 equiv standard.
- HAMMER (STP identity): kick+snare unison 16ths, china/open-hat 8ths. 180-230 16ths.
- BOMB (climax blast): kick EVERY 32nd (both feet), snare 16ths unison w/ even
  kicks, china on-beats / crash off-8ths alternating. Lorna choruses. 18-19 kicks/s.
- GRAVITY (flex topper): snare every 32nd in strong/weak PAIRS (down ~100 / up ~78),
  whole lane 15-40 vel under backbeat, kick 16ths, free hand ride 8ths.
- Kick-only 32nd burst (2 beats) ANNOUNCES gear changes, ends on target downbeat.

## Verified structure (Pain Remains III arc)
verse trad blast -> kick-wall-under-chugs tension (snare only on riff accents) ->
BOMB at climax (+ choir/key change) -> gravity/hammer 2-4 bar flex -> half-time cut.
Outros: stepped tempo ramps DOWN (Prison of Flesh: 110->100->85->70->60).
Gear-up jumps exist too (Pain Remains II 125->135 finale).

## Programming realism at 280-330 equiv
- Blast snare: 85-105 band, width <=15 (layer flicker above that), periodic 4-hit
  contour (e.g. 100-92-96-90), NOT random. Backbeats stay 115-127.
- Kicks: FLAT 110-120 (+-3). Grid-locked, ZERO humanize (triggered genre sound).
- Unison hits (hammer/bomb): sample-accurate together, no jitter.
- Blast snare jitter <= +-2-5ms, prefer consistent lean; cymbals loosest (+-5-10ms).
- Cymbal map: trad=ride 8ths; bomb=china/crash alternating; hammer=china wash;
  gravity=ride 8ths (one hand is rolling); phrase starts stack china+crash.
- Fills at speed: quads (toms ride ON TOP of continuing kick wall), sextuplet
  snare ruff into crash at turnarounds, 32nd stutters; <=1 beat inside blasts.
- Kick sound at walls: single consistent sample, click 3-6kHz carries rhythm,
  tails must die <50ms or the wall muds.

## Benchmark tempos (transcription-derived)
Baba Yaga 195 main (not 285); To the Hellfire 130 session / 32nds = 260 equiv,
mid-section 32nd-triplet kicks @105 = 21/s = 315 equiv; Sun//Eater 140 = 280;
SotS Amongst the Low & Empty 140-143 = 280-286; Malady 165 w/ 32nd bursts = 330;
BoS Lifeblood 160 = 320 bursts; Mental Cruelty Symphony of a Dying Star 220 16ths.

## How v3 applies this
Session stays at song tempos; blast sections tempo-map UP (blast_a 160, peak 152)
and use 16th grids there = 32nd grids at 80/76 half-time = 320/304 equivalents,
landing on the same absolute rates as the benchmarks (trad combined 21.3 hits/s,
bomb kicks 21.3/s, gravity roll 24/s). Kick-led trad, flat grid-locked kicks,
narrow-band snare ladders, gear-change kick bursts, stepped outro ramp 100->88->80->72.
