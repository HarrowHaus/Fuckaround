# Netherwalker research brief (song 3 input)

One relevant band: Ocala FL "medieval fantasy deathcore" trio (Grayson Yearty,
Alex Calver, Mauricio Cortez Bowles), formed 2021 as solo project. Debut album
Odyssey of Respair (Nov 2025, 14-track knight-quest concept). Breakout:
"Tavern" (2024) — fantasy tavern piano intro -> deathcore -> one of 2025's
most-memed breakdowns. Influences: Infant Annihilator, Necrophagist, Dark Funeral.

Fingerprint: crushing chugs/breakdowns alternating with technical passages and
blackened tremolo; melody over dissonance; constant orchestral/choir/piano
layers mixed to SUPPORT guitars; voice-acted lore interludes; theatrical vocal
variety (gutturals/shrieks/gurgles); compact songs 2:47-4:10; whiplash
dynamics (blasts vs huge half-time drops); heroic melodic leads over blasts
("Crystal Knight" mode).

## Archspire x Netherwalker hybrid brief
- Archspire: hyper-speed precision verses, burst riffing, sweeps/taps, lead
  bass, machine-gun vocal cadence, start-stop unison stabs, bone-dry tightness.
- Netherwalker: symphonic/cinematic frame (orchestra, choir, tavern piano cold
  open), black-metal tremolo/gallop bridges, huge half-time breakdown(s),
  medieval quest narrative, fun played straight.
- Target: ~4 min, tech verses at 290-310 equivalent framed by fantasy
  cinematics, one gigantic tavern-shaking breakdown, heroic lead melody
  recapitulated, compact and theatrical.
