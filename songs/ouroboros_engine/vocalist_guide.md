# OUROBOROS ENGINE — Vocalist's Map

**The concept:** The recursive song. One seed riff, evolved against the mined
corpus constraints (never copying it — every pattern is verified ≥2 edits
from every corpus mask), then transformed section by section: each part of
the song is a development of the previous part, and the seed returns alone
at the end. The full lineage with fitness curves is in `docs/tabs-song6.txt`.

## Writing notes
- Lyric lane: recursion, self-consumption, becoming your own ancestor.
  The song's structure IS the lyric prompt — everything returns transformed.
- The opening line and the tail line should be the same words, delivered
  differently (clean start / destroyed end).
- Every riff you hear is genuinely new: none of the patterns exist in the
  39-song corpus — the corpus only taught the engine what "playable and
  idiomatic" means.
