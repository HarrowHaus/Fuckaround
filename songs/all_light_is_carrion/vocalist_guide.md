# ALL LIGHT IS CARRION — Vocalist's Map

**The concept:** The reference-locked song. Three targets, chosen by the
band: Sun//Eater (blast + tremolo melody), Amongst the Low & Empty (slam),
Second Death (doom finale). The engine anchored to their mined laws
(open 0.49 / pedal 0.82), never their bars. The seed riff was picked by a
machine critic from three rendered candidates.
