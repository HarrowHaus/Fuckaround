# CURB GOSPEL — Vocalist's Map

**The concept:** Full 2026 underground dialect (docs/17-20). No orchestra, no
cleans, no mercy. Every riff mined from the 2018-2026 corpus — fretted
half-step pedals, displacement bounce, Knocked Loose tritone skronk. The
breakdown you hear at 0:39 is the front-loaded one; the one at 1:58 is the
earned one, and it drops the whole band to F (Whitechapel gear-change law).
**Tuning:** Drop G 7-string (final section sounds in drop F) · **Runtime:** 2:45
**Vocals:** lows/mids/highs, rap-cadence delivery welcome in the bounce
sections, NO cleans, NO sung hooks. Track against
`mix/curb_gospel_vocal_ready.wav`.

## Writing notes
- Lyric lane: concrete and street-level, not cosmic — betrayal, pavement,
  hypocrisy of the pulpit ("curb gospel" = the sermon you get face-down).
  No fantasy vocabulary; that was song 4.
- The 0:39 breakdown's first line doubles as the merch line. 4-6 words.
- Reference flows: Suffocate (Knocked Loose) for the skronk sections,
  The G Code (PeelingFlesh) for bounce cadence, Hymns in Dissonance
  (Whitechapel) for the drop-F finale.
- "Blegh" is permitted exactly once, at 1:58, ironically and at full
  commitment (docs/18: ironic-but-indispensable).
