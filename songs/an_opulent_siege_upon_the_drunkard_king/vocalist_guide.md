# AN OPULENT SIEGE UPON THE DRUNKARD KING — Vocalist's Map

**The concept:** Netherwalker's fantasy demoted to guitar melody inside a 2007
MySpace-deathcore frame. Knights, a drunkard king, a siege — delivered like
*Dead in My Arms*-era brutality. **Every riff was generated from the mined
era canon and is readable as tab in `docs/tabs-song4.txt`.**
**Tuning:** Drop A# · **Runtime:** 2:26 (MVP-length) · **Vocals:** lows/highs/
pig squeals/gang shouts ONLY — no whispers, no gurgle-theatrics (scene law)

## Writing notes

  king too drunk to defend his crown. Long ironic song title = era-correct.
- The gang-shout line should be 4-6 words, chantable by a VFW hall.
- Reference vocal flows: Unanswered (verse), MVP (breakdown), Entombment of a
  Machine (squeal call-and-response).
